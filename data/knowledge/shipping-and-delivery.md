Orders placed before 12:00 CET on business days usually leave our Bergen warehouse within 1–2 business days. During launches and holiday periods, dispatch can take up to 3 business days.

Norwegian standard delivery takes 2–4 business days after dispatch and costs 89 NOK. Delivery is free when the order value is at least 1,200 NOK after discounts. Express delivery takes 1–2 business days and costs 179 NOK. Remote areas can require one additional business day.

When the carrier scans the parcel, we email a tracking link. Tracking can take up to 12 hours to show movement. Delivery estimates are targets, not guarantees, and exclude weekends and public holidays.
