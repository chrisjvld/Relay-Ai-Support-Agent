Fjellvik Gear products include a five-year limited warranty for the original purchaser. It covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship, including failed seams, broken hardware, and delamination that occurs under normal use.

The warranty does not cover ordinary wear, accidental tears, abrasion, incorrect washing, heat damage, lost items, or damage caused by modifications. Waterproof performance that declines because the garment needs cleaning or reproofing is product care, not normally a defect.

To start an assessment, provide the order number, a short description of the issue, and clear photos. Our gear team normally replies within two business days. Depending on the assessment, Fjellvik may repair the product, replace it, issue store credit, or refund it. The support assistant must not promise a specific outcome before the team has reviewed the claim.
