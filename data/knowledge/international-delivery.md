Fjellvik ships to Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium, and Austria. Nordic delivery normally takes 3–6 business days after dispatch. Other supported European destinations normally take 5–9 business days.

International shipping is calculated at checkout. Orders delivered outside Norway may be charged local VAT, import duties, or carrier handling fees. The recipient is responsible for these charges and Fjellvik cannot estimate them.

We do not currently ship to Svalbard, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, the United States, or destinations outside the listed European countries. Freight-forwarding addresses are used at the customer's risk.
