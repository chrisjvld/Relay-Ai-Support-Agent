Fjellvik uses European sizing. Most jackets and midlayers have a regular fit with enough room for one base layer. Choose your usual size for a standard fit or size up if you expect to wear a thick insulated layer underneath. Our unisex shells use men's measurements; many women prefer one size smaller.

Lofoten Hiking Trousers fit true to size through the waist, with an articulated regular cut through the thigh. If you are between waist sizes, choose the larger size. Trouser inseams marked Short, Regular, and Long differ by 5 cm.

Measure around the fullest part of the chest and the natural waist while wearing a thin layer. Product-specific measurements on each product page take priority over this general guidance.
