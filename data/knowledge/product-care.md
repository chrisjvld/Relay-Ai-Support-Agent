updatedAt: 2026-10-09
---

Close all zips, loosen drawcords, empty pockets, and wash technical shells at 30°C on a gentle cycle. Use a small amount of liquid technical cleaner. Do not use powder detergent, fabric softener, bleach, or dry cleaning because they can damage the membrane and water-repellent finish.

Air dry first. If the care label permits it, tumble dry on low for 20 minutes to reactivate the durable water-repellent finish. When water no longer beads after washing and low heat, apply a suitable wash-in or spray-on reproofer.

Wash merino inside out at 30°C on a wool cycle and dry it flat. Down products need a down-specific wash and a low tumble dry with dryer balls until completely dry. Always follow the individual garment label when it differs from this guide.
