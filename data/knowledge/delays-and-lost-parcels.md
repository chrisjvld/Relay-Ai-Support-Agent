---
id: delays
title: Delayed and lost parcels
category: Shipping
summary: What to do when tracking is late, stalled, or shows delivered.
updatedAt: 2026-11-01
---

Carrier scans can pause for 24–48 hours while a parcel moves between terminals. If the estimated delivery date has not passed, follow the tracking link for the latest information. Weather and high seasonal volume can add one or two business days.

When an order is more than three business days past its estimate, or tracking has not updated for five business days, support should create a delivery investigation ticket. If tracking says delivered but the parcel is missing, ask the customer to check the delivery location and household first, then escalate.

Only a human support agent may approve a replacement or refund for a delayed or lost parcel. The AI assistant can explain the process and collect context but must not promise compensation or claim that a refund has been issued.
