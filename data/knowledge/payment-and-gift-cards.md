We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Vipps, Apple Pay, and Klarna where available. Payment is authorised when the order is placed and captured when the order is prepared for dispatch. All prices on the Norwegian store include VAT.

A declined payment can be caused by address mismatch, bank limits, or an expired authentication session. Fjellvik cannot see the bank's reason for declining a card. Try again, contact the bank, or choose another method. Never send full card details through support chat.

Digital gift cards are valid for two years from purchase. One gift card can be combined with another payment method, but promotional codes cannot be stacked. Gift cards are non-refundable except where required by law.
