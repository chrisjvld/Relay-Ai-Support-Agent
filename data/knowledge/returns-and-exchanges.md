## Standard return window

Fjellvik Gear accepts returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be clean, unwashed, and in resalable condition with the original tags attached. Footwear may be tried on indoors only. Return shipping costs 79 NOK and is deducted from the refund unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong product.

## Used outdoor clothing

We cannot accept a normal return for a jacket or other garment that has been worn outdoors, washed, stained, or damaged through use. Trying a jacket on indoors to check the fit is fine. A jacket used on a hike is considered used and is not eligible for a change-of-mind return.

If a used jacket appears faulty—for example, a seam has failed or the membrane is leaking despite correct care—it may qualify for a warranty assessment instead. Contact support with the order number, a description, and clear photos. Approval is not automatic and support agents cannot promise a refund before inspection.

## Refund timing and exchanges

Refunds go to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after our Bergen returns team approves the item. Banks can take another 2–5 business days to display the credit. We do not offer direct online exchanges; return the original item and place a new order for the preferred size or colour.
